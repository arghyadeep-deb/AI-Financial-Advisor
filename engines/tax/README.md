# Archived salary arithmetic, partial feature 004

FY2024-25 / AY2025-26, new regime, one employer, resident individual,
salary only, gross salary at most INR50 lakh, standard deduction only.
Inputs are already reconciled salary figures; no bank-income inference.
Other categories, deductions/exemptions, source conflicts and adjacent periods
are refused. Missing withholding leaves settlement unavailable. Sections
115BAC/87A, salary deduction and 288A/B are pinned in `rules.py` with official
references checked 2026-11-07. No current-period claim or production approval.
Refresh by a new rule version; never overwrite this archived pack. Pure engine:
no database, document parser, API, tax export, filing or provider integration.
Form16/AIS/26AS identity and period reconciliation remains with feature 004.
Interest, fees and statutory validation remain unimplemented; this is not a
complete tax draft. Golden cases are synthetic and hand calculated.
